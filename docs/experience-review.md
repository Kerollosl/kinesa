# Kinesa experience review — 0.9

The companions now use articulated 2.5D rigs with separately painted heads, torsos, wings and limbs. Head curiosity, breathing, wing flex, paws, hands and Mattie’s floating halo run continuously. Local painted eyelids close smoothly; blinking no longer swaps the entire illustration. The same Gospel identities and softly shaded cartoon style remain throughout the app.

A shared animation scheduler drives damped joint springs. Greetings and pointer attention preserve current position and velocity, including repeated taps. Gesture targets release back into the idle motion already in progress. Scene characters keep the same DOM instances instead of remounting on each reaction, and competing CSS bobs/hops were removed. Offscreen characters and hidden tabs suspend; pause freezes phase and joints, and resume avoids wall-clock catch-up. Reduced motion shows a static semantic pose and remains responsive to deliberate interactions.

The artwork has painted underlap at shoulders and necks, fixed foot grounding, face-focused small icons, and lossless WebP delivery. Visible pixels were checked against the PNG originals after compression; the three animation textures are 41% smaller with identical visible channel values.

The review also inspected empty and earned collection states, incorrect feedback, completed results, and dialogs. Automated scans supplement the visual and interaction review; they are not a substitute for a real-device accessibility assessment.
