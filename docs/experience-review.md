# Kinesa experience review — 0.13

The companions restore the original illustrated designs: Leo’s fluffy orange mane and warm smile, Lou’s stocky calf silhouette and ivory horns, Juno’s expressive white head and layered brown feathers, and Mattie’s curls, lavender tunic and halo. Their familiar faces and soft dimensional shading remain in the painted artwork. The cast uses articulated illustrations, not procedural 3D meshes.

The paintings now use deformable texture meshes. Head turns curve the original face across a rounded surface, the torso bends above planted feet, feather spans curl, and mane movement follows the head’s momentum. Small brow and smile changes stay within the original facial features. Authored pose curves coordinate the whole performance: weight commitment precedes a one-handed greeting, the torso and head settle into a wing hug, and two unequal jumps release and regain ground contact. Independent damped springs preserve momentum when these performances interrupt each other.

The crew now stays busy through a behavior director: overlapping glances, wing stretches, foot shuffles and gentle sways keep the idle varied over longer sessions. Each character has a different rhythm and energy. Dance toggles a continuous groove; greetings, celebrations and wing hugs layer over it. Hops include anticipation and soft landings, with hearts or sparkles for deliberate reactions. The original head artwork stays attached throughout. Controls support keyboard use, and a long hold sends love while a scroll or canceled touch cancels the hold.

A shared animation scheduler updates visible companions without React rerenders on every frame. The WebGL surface uses the original painted textures; Canvas 2D draws the same soft mesh when WebGL is unavailable. The SVG artwork stays usable if canvas is unavailable, GPU initialization fails or a context is lost. Context restoration reconnects the existing rig. Scene pause preserves joints, skin and effects while deliberate practice spins remain available. Hidden tabs and dialogs also suspend active practice-wheel transitions and defer their results until resume. Offscreen characters suspend. Portraits and reduced motion use the original SVG artwork. The implementation runs in the existing web app.

## Focused play and companion placement — November 2026

- Home uses short mode descriptions and an optional icon toolbar for companion actions. The illustration has its own full-width row, without negative margins under the heading.
- Setup now centers the form and roster. Repeated promotional panels, helper paragraphs, and deal confirmations have been removed; local-play and practice-bot distinctions remain explicit.
- The active companion occupies a clipped slot beside the turn heading. The wheel and spin button have no character overlays. Feedback uses a separate small companion slot above the next-turn action.
- Correctness, score and the next action appear immediately. Explanations and source notes remain available in “Why this answer?”; the category key is also optional.
- Discover keeps its topic picker and sample question, with less repeated description. Collection uses lock/check symbols for badge state and retains its local-progress explanation.
- Browser regression coverage checks wheel/answer hit targets, companion separation and optional explanations at 360, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels. Managed browser inspection is unavailable in this executor; GitHub CI runs these checks alongside the existing end-to-end suite.
